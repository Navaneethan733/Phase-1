Our Story

Founded in 2010, Stackly began as a small family kitchen with a big dream: to share the authentic flavors of India with the world. What started as a humble venture has now become one of the most beloved Indian food destinations, serving thousands of satisfied customers every day.

Our Journey

From a small kitchen to becoming a household name in Indian cuisine

2010

The Beginning

Stackly was founded by Chef Rajesh Kumar in his home kitchen, serving authentic biryani to neighbors and friends in Delhi.

2013

First Restaurant

Opened our first brick-and-mortar restaurant in Connaught Place, Delhi, quickly gaining popularity for our authentic flavors.

2016

Delivery Service Launch

Introduced our delivery service, bringing Stackly's authentic Indian cuisine to homes across Delhi NCR.

2019

Expansion

Expanded to 5 cities across India and launched our mobile app for easier ordering and tracking.

2022

Awards & Recognition

Received the "Best Indian Restaurant Chain" award and reached 5000+ daily happy customers.

2025

Going Global

Launched international delivery services and expanded our menu to include regional specialties from across India.

Our Mission & Vision

Driven by passion, guided by values

Our Mission

To deliver authentic Indian cuisine using traditional recipes and the freshest ingredients, creating memorable dining experiences that celebrate India's rich culinary heritage while adapting to modern lifestyles.

Our Vision

To become India's most loved food brand globally, recognized for culinary excellence, innovation, and sustainable practices while maintaining our commitment to authentic flavors and exceptional customer service.

Our Promise

Every meal we serve is crafted with care, using recipes passed down through generations. We promise consistency in quality, taste, and service, ensuring every customer feels the warmth of Indian hospitality.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We stay true to traditional recipes and cooking methods, preserving the authentic taste of Indian cuisine that has been perfected over generations.

Quality

Customer First

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We listen, adapt, and go above and beyond to exceed expectations.

Passion

Innovation

While honoring tradition, we continuously innovate to improve our services and customer experience.
