How Can We Help?

Browse through our frequently asked questions to find the information you need about our restaurant, ordering, delivery, and more.

How can I place an order with Stackly? +

You can place an order with Stackly in three convenient ways:

  • Online through our website (click "Order Now" button)
  • Via our mobile app (coming soon!)
  • By calling us directly at +91 9876543210
  • In-person at our restaurant location

We recommend ordering online for the fastest experience and to track your order in real-time.

What are your opening hours? +

Our restaurant hours are:

  • Monday - Friday: 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Saturday - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 11:00 PM
  • Public Holidays: 12:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Delivery hours may vary slightly during peak seasons. Kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time for last orders.

What areas do you deliver to? +

We currently deliver within a 10km radius of our restaurant located at 123 Spice Street, India. You can check if your address is within our delivery zone by entering your postal code during the checkout process on our website.

We're constantly expanding our delivery areas, so if you're currently outside our zone, check back with us in the future!

How long does delivery usually take? +

Our average delivery time is 30-45 minutes from when you place your order. However, during peak hours (weekends 6-9 PM, holidays) delivery may take up to 60 minutes.

We offer express delivery (under 30 minutes) for orders placed during non-peak hours. You'll receive an estimated delivery time when you place your order, along with real-time tracking.

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options? +

Yes! We have an extensive selection of vegetarian and vegan dishes. Our menu clearly marks all vegetarian (🟢) and vegan (🌱) options. Some of our popular plant-based dishes include:

  • Vegetable Biryani
  • Chana Masala (vegan)
  • Aloo Gobi (vegan)
  • Dal Makhani (vegetarian)
  • Paneer Butter Masala (vegetarian)

We can also customize most dishes to accommodate dietary preferences - just let us know in the special instructions when ordering.

Are your dishes very spicy? Can I adjust the spice level? +

We offer customizable spice levels for all our dishes! When ordering, you can select from:

  • Mild: Perfect for those who prefer subtle flavors
  • Medium: Our standard level with a balanced kick
  • Hot: For spice lovers who enjoy the heat
  • Extra Hot: Only for the brave!

If you forget to specify, we'll prepare your dish at our standard medium level. Our chefs are experts at balancing flavors at every spice level.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept various payment methods for your convenience:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Digital Wallets (PayTM, Google Pay, PhonePe)
  • UPI Payments
  • Net Banking
  • Cash on Delivery (available for orders under ₹2000)

All online payments are secured with 128-bit SSL encryption for your safety.

Is there a minimum order amount for delivery? +

Yes, we have a minimum order amount of ₹300 for delivery. This helps us maintain our fast delivery service while keeping our prices competitive.

For orders below ₹300, you're welcome to place a takeaway order and pick it up from our restaurant. We also occasionally waive the minimum for first-time customers - check our promotions page for current offers!

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it? +

You can modify or cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it. After that, our kitchen begins preparation and we cannot guarantee changes.

To request a modification or cancellation:

  1. Call us immediately at +91 9876543210
  2. Provide your order number (sent to your email/phone)
  3. Our customer service team will assist you

If your order has already been dispatched for delivery, we won't be able to cancel it, but we'll do our best to accommodate any concerns.

Do you offer catering for events and parties? +

Absolutely! We offer catering services for events ranging from intimate gatherings to large corporate functions. Our catering menu includes:

  • Customizable buffet setups
  • Individual meal boxes
  • Live food counters (chaat, dosa, etc.)
  • Dessert stations

For catering inquiries, please contact our events team at catering@stackly.com or call +91 9876543211 at least 72 hours before your event.
